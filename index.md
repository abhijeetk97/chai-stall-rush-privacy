# Privacy Policy — Chai Stall Rush

**Effective date:** 22 October 2026
**Developer:** Chowk Games
**App:** Chai Stall Rush (Android package `com.chowkgames.chaistallrush`)

Chai Stall Rush is a single-player game. This policy explains what information the game handles. The short version: **we do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.**

## Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no in-app tracking. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files, and it does not request the Internet permission.

## Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (for example coins, unlocked items, settings and preferences) in the app's private storage on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent anywhere. Android may include app data in your own device or Google account backup if you have backups turned on; that is controlled by your device settings and Google's policies, not by us.

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

## Third parties

The game does not include third-party advertising, analytics or social SDKs, and it does not share data with any third party.

## Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region).

## Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change (for example, if we add ads or online features), we will update this page and the effective date above before the change ships.
